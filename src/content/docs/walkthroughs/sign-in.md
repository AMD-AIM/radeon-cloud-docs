**When:** Your first visit, or a new browser or device.

![Animated walkthrough: sign in](/radeon-cloud-docs/walkthroughs/en/sign-in.gif)

## Steps

1. Open the site and click **Sign in**.
2. Choose **Login with Email** (or **Login with GitHub**).
3. Enter your email address and click **Send code**.
4. Wait for the 6-digit code in your inbox.
5. Enter the code and click **Verify & sign in**.
6. You're signed in, and the launch panel is ready.

## Common pitfalls

- The code goes to the address you typed and expires in a few minutes. No mail? Check the spam folder, then send a new code.
