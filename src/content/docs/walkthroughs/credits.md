**When:** Before you launch, or when a launch is refused.

![Animated walkthrough: credits](/radeon-cloud-docs/walkthroughs/en/credits.gif)

## Steps

1. Your balance is shown in the account menu.
2. **Credits & Usage**: each GPU uses 1 pt per started hour, from ready until deleted.
3. Have a code? Click **Redeem credits**, paste it and redeem. Credits are added at once.
4. On the launch panel, the auto-delete time caps the cost, and the panel shows the most this instance can use.
5. With 0 pts the balance shows 0.
6. Without enough credits for the chosen size, the launch is refused.

## Common pitfalls

- The clock runs from ready until the instance is deleted, whether or not you use it.
- The auto-delete time sets the most an instance can cost. Pick the shortest that fits your work.
- With too few credits the launch is refused (“Not enough credits”). Redeem first, then start again.
