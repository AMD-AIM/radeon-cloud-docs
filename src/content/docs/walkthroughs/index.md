Each step is a short animated walkthrough of the real console, with the easy-to-miss points marked in orange. Follow them in order for your first session.

1. [Sign in](/radeon-cloud-docs/walkthroughs/sign-in/): the code expires in minutes; check spam.
2. [Check your credits](/radeon-cloud-docs/walkthroughs/credits/): 1 pt per GPU per started hour until the instance is deleted.
3. [Launch a notebook](/radeon-cloud-docs/walkthroughs/launch/): choose **Persistent /workspace** under Customize to keep files.
4. [Work in JupyterLab](/radeon-cloud-docs/walkthroughs/jupyterlab/): only `/workspace` can be kept.
5. [Connect with SSH (optional)](/radeon-cloud-docs/walkthroughs/ssh/): add your key **before** you launch.
6. [Destroy and keep your work](/radeon-cloud-docs/walkthroughs/keep-your-work/): only persistent `/workspace` is kept.
7. [Call models with Token Lab](/radeon-cloud-docs/walkthroughs/token-factory/): leave `max_tokens` room; check `/v1/usage` for limits.
