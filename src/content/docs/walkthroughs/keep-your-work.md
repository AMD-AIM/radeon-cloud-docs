**When:** You're done for now but want to keep your results.

![Animated walkthrough: destroy and keep your work](/radeon-cloud-docs/walkthroughs/en/keep-your-work.gif)

## Steps

1. The card shows when the instance is deleted automatically, used or not. Click **Extend** if you need longer.
2. Save your work in `/workspace`, the folder that can be kept.
3. Click **Destroy instance**. The confirmation says temporary data is deleted, persistent `/workspace` data is kept, and it can't be undone.
4. The instance is gone and stops using credits.
5. Launch again with **Persistent /workspace** under Customize.
6. Your file is back in `/workspace`.

## Common pitfalls

- Only `/workspace` is kept, and only if you launched with **Persistent /workspace**. Anything saved elsewhere is gone.
- Destroying can't be undone, and the auto-delete time ends the instance anyway. **Extend** it if you need longer.

More detail: [Destroy an instance](/radeon-cloud-docs/guides/destroy/)
